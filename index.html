    Relay score is renewal time, account spend and account health, averaged. Higher is better, so the book opens on its weakest accounts. Tap a row or a category.

    Where the record lies ·
    How the score works

    One number per account, zero to one hundred, higher is better. A high score is an account in good standing you can leave alone; a low score is an account that needs you. Three inputs averaged, and every account shows all three with the reason behind each, so you can see which one is dragging.

    Renewal is runway: zero days scores zero, six months or more scores one hundred, straight line between. Spend is contract value ranked inside this book, smallest zero, largest one hundred, median fifty, so one huge account cannot squash the rest. Health is the only input that says something is wrong: it starts at one hundred and loses up to 25 for a stale next step (Low trust 25, Medium 12), up to 25 for usage (falling 25, flat 10), 15 for tickets or souring support sentiment, 20 for service failures.

    Reading it. Under 40, this week. 40 to 69, this month. 70 and up, leave it alone. Trust is separate: how far the record has drifted from what the humans wrote. Category says what kind of attention: Run now, Protect, Expand or Watch.
