How the score works

One number per account, zero to one hundred, higher is better. A high score is an account in good standing you can leave alone; a low score is an account that needs you. It is three inputs averaged, and every row shows all three so you can see which one is dragging.

Renewal is runway. Zero days scores zero, six months or more scores one hundred, straight line in between. Past half a year timing is not the problem, so it stops there.

Spend is contract value as a rank inside this book, not a raw scale: smallest account zero, largest one hundred, median fifty. One huge account cannot squash everyone else.

Health is the only input that says something is wrong. It starts at one hundred and loses: up to 25 for a stale next step (Low trust 25, Medium 12, High 0), up to 25 for usage (falling 25, flat 10, growing 0), 15 for open tickets or negative support sentiment, 20 for service failures.

Reading it. Under 40, something is wrong and the clock is running: this week. 40 to 69, worth a look this month. 70 and up, in good standing.

Trust is a separate judgement: how far the CRM record has drifted from what the humans wrote in the notes. Category says what kind of attention: Run now, Protect, Expand or Watch.
